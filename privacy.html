Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

FaceOverTime respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the FaceOverTime application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use FaceOverTime:

  • Account information – your name, email address, or sign-in credentials if you create an account.
  • Selfies and photos – the daily selfies you take through the app, processed locally or on secure servers to generate your time-lapse timeline.
  • Usage data – anonymised analytics such as session duration, feature usage, and crash reports.
  • Device information – device model, operating system version and app version, used to keep the app working reliably.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve the FaceOverTime service.
  • Generate your time-lapse video timeline from your daily selfies.
  • Manage your account and any subscriptions.
  • Detect, prevent and fix bugs, abuse or security issues.
  • Understand how the app is used to improve features and user experience.

3. Selfies & Photo Data

Your selfies are the core of FaceOverTime. We treat them with care. Photos are processed securely and stored only as long as needed to provide the service. You may delete your photos and account data at any time through the app's settings or by contacting us directly.

4. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your selfies or personal information. We may rely on third-party service providers for hosting, analytics, crash reporting and payment processing. These providers access information only as necessary to provide their services and are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest. No online service can guarantee complete security, but we work hard to keep your information safe.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account we will remove your personal data and selfies, subject to any legal or technical retention requirements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at contact@newiracraft.com.

8. Children's Privacy

FaceOverTime is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal data we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or on our website. Continued use of FaceOverTime after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at contact@newiracraft.com.
